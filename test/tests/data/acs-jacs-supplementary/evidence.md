Observed 2026-09-05 in the Codex in-app browser on ACS Publications. These are browser DOM observations and do not claim a successful Connector-to-Desktop byte-transfer run.

- `https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article/148/28/29684/5206327/Structural-Engineering-of-Cyanine-Dyes-to-Access` (DOI `10.1021/jacs.5c22031`, article dated July 10, 2026): the loaded page exposed `meta[name="citation_doi"] = 10.1021/jacs.5c22031`, a `Supporting Information` section, and a direct anchor with `data-doctype="dataSupplementDoc"`, `aria-label="Download sifile1"`, and href `https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article-supplement/5206327/pdf/ja5c22031_si_001/`. The page also exposed an article-linked Figshare record at `https://figshare.com/articles/journal_contribution/Structural_Engineering_of_Cyanine_Dyes_to_Access_Shortwave_Infrared-Emissive_J_Aggregates/32957544`.
- `https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article-abstract/147/46/42330/3739766/Higher-Order-Cooperativity-Emerging-from-the?redirectedFrom=fulltext` (DOI `10.1021/jacs.5c10869`, article dated November 06, 2025): the abstract page exposed the same observed `data-doctype="dataSupplementDoc"` download anchor at `https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article-supplement/3739766/pdf/ja5c10869_si_001/`, plus the article-linked Figshare record `https://figshare.com/articles/journal_contribution/Higher_Order_Cooperativity_Emerging_from_the_Helicoidal_Growth_of_Curved_Supramolecular_Polymers/30559606`. The page said the article was available to purchase, so this is a positive SI discovery control with an access-limited main article.
- `https://pubs.acs.org/jacsat/article-abstract/148/34/36275/5323064/Design-of-Chemically-Programmable-Nanomaterials?redirectedFrom=fulltext` (DOI `10.1021/jacs.6c09388`, article dated August 24, 2026): the Perspective page exposed no `Supporting Information` section, no `data-doctype="dataSupplementDoc"` anchor, and no article-linked Figshare anchor. This is the verified no-SI control; the page itself was available to purchase and displayed ordinary ACS access messaging, not a CAPTCHA or challenge.

The production fixtures preserve the exact observed article URLs and direct SI routes. Figshare manifest, API failure, malformed response, signed-query, duplicate-name, and mismatched-DOI cases in `ACSJACS SupplementaryTest.mjs` are synthetic helper/API responses; they do not represent live file-transfer evidence. No CAPTCHA or challenge bypass was attempted.

The ACS Figshare fallback is deliberately bounded to three discovery requests per article: one search plus at most two unique detail records. A search response matching more than two Figshare records is logged as a limit result and falls back to the already observed page SI links; it is not silently truncated. Detail URLs are requested only when they are plain HTTP(S) `api.figshare.com/v2/articles/<numeric-id>` URLs without credentials, ports, query strings, or fragments. Oversized, malformed, or DOI-mismatched synthetic manifests therefore preserve the page SI and metadata.
